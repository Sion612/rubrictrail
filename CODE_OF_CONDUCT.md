# Code of conduct

RubricTrail contributors are expected to make participation welcoming, safe and
useful for people of different backgrounds and experience levels.

Be respectful, assume good faith, give actionable feedback, credit others, and
protect private educational material. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
doxxing, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, and publishing another
person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
needed to protect the community. Reports should use the private path described in
[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) when they contain sensitive information. Enforcement
decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid retaliation.
